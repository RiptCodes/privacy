ResaleTally privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026.

ResaleTally collects no data. It has no account, no server, no analytics and no adverts.

What the extension reads

When you are signed in to Vinted in your own browser and open your own sales pages, the extension reads the sale details shown on that page (item title, sale price, date, fees and postage where shown). It does this only on Vinted pages, only in your browser.

Where it is stored

Everything is saved in chrome.storage.local, inside your own browser profile. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else. The extension makes no network requests of its own. Removing the extension erases the data. The "Clear demo" button removes the sample sales.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer data, because we never receive it. CSV files you export are saved to your own computer.

Not tax advice

ResaleTally gives general guidance based on published HMRC rules. It is not tax advice. Check gov.uk or ask an adviser about your own situation.

Not affiliated

ResaleTally is an independent tool and is not made by, or connected to, Vinted or HMRC.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so and the extension's store listing will be updated first.
