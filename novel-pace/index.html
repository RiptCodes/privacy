Novel Pace privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026.

Novel Pace collects no data. It has no account, no server, no analytics and no adverts.

What the extension reads

Novel Pace counts the words you add to Google Docs each day. All counting happens in your browser. The text of your documents is read only to count it, is never kept, and never leaves your browser.

Where it is stored

Your word counts, goal and settings are kept on your device in the browser's extension storage and are removed when you uninstall the extension. The extension makes no network requests of its own.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data, because we never receive any.

Not affiliated

Novel Pace is an independent tool and is not made by, or connected to, Google.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so and the extension's store listing will be updated first.
